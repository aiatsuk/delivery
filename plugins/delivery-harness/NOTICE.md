# Ownership and upstream notices

This repository records the owner's requested integration of existing workflows.
Source commits and integration decisions are recorded in the Delivery repository's
submodules, lock and review records. Those records are not a blanket license grant.

Orchestrate supplies an MIT license; its notice is retained in
`notices/orchestrate-LICENSE`. The inspected spec-driven-development,
product-driven-development and factory source revisions have no top-level license
file. No license is invented for those sources or the combined plug-in. Review
ownership and applicable permissions before third-party redistribution.

Only public-safe integration code, fixtures and documentation are included. Native
session archives, local verification transcripts and private platform snapshots
are not part of this package.
